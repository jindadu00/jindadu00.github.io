Robotics researcher · Shanghai

Jinda Du 杜劲达

Data collection and robot learning for contact-rich dexterous manipulation.

I am a Ph.D. student at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), advised by Prof. Xiangyang Zhu. I work in the Soft Robotics and Biodesign (SRB) Lab, led by Prof. Guoying Gu, with research guidance from Prof. Jieji Ren.

My research focuses on data collection and robot learning for contact-rich dexterous manipulation, with an emphasis on integrating force and tactile feedback into vision-language-action (VLA) models and world-action models (WAMs). I am currently an intern at the Embodied Intelligence Center of Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, working under the guidance of Qiaojun Yu.

I received my B.Eng. in Engineering Mechanics through SJTU's Zhiyuan Honors Program, graduating first in my major, with a minor in Computer Science and Technology.
